# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-10-24

First release for Minecraft 26.3 (Fabric). Your workstations, straight from your inventory.

- Middle-click a crafting table, stonecutter, loom, cartography table, smithing table, grindstone
  or anvil in your inventory to use it without placing it, from your inventory or any container
  screen. In creative, hold `Ctrl` (`Cmd` on macOS).
- Middle-click the station again to go back to the chest or inventory you opened it from.
- The station you are using stays in its slot, marked with a small padlock; spares in the same
  stack stay free to move.
- The recipe book never uses the station in use as an ingredient.
- The grindstone gives you its experience and the anvil wears out like a placed one.
- Each station plays its placing sound when it opens, with a settings screen to turn it off
  (Mod Menu).
- Works with Shulker Toolkit's crafting from boxes and its middle-click windows.
- Server owners can turn each station off for other players.
- Available in English, Español (España), Español (México), Português (Brasil), Português
  (Portugal), Français, Deutsch, Italiano, Nederlands, Polski, Türkçe, Русский, Українська,
  简体中文, 繁體中文, 日本語 and 한국어.
